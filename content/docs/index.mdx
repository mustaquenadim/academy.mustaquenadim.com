Most courses hand you a definition and a diagram, then wonder why none of it sticks.
This academy is different. Every lesson starts with a **problem you can feel** — an app
that's crawling, a system that keeps falling over, a task eating your whole afternoon —
and then we *discover* the solution together, the same way the people who invented it did.

By the time we name the thing ("this is a hash table", "this is a load balancer"), you
already understand *why* it has to exist. The name becomes a label for something you own,
not one more term to memorize.
